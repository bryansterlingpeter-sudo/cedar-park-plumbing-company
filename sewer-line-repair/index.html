Sewer Line Repair in Cedar Park, TX

Sewer line checks and repairs for backups, odors, and wet ground in Cedar Park.

Call (512) 986-2512 Ask a question

In older homes across Cedar Park, Anderson Mill, Brushy Creek, and Jollyville, we check buried sewer pipe where clay, cast iron, or aging plastic joints may have shifted, cracked, or separated. Tree roots can enter an open joint and hold paper and waste inside the line.

We also inspect newer properties after ground movement, construction debris, or a damaged cleanout cap affects the pipe route from the house. A sewer line can develop a low spot that holds wastewater instead of carrying it away.

We do not recommend leaving a suspected sewer line fault to wait. A restriction or break can progress from a slow fixture to sewage backing into a bathroom, and a leaking pipe can wash soil away from its route.

Sewage odor and backups

Customers call us about a toilet that gurgles while a washing machine empties, a tub that fills with dark water, or a shower drain that bubbles. We also investigate sewage odor outdoors, wet ground near the pipe, and wastewater coming up at a floor drain.

Those signs can come from roots, a sagging pipe section, a broken joint, or a collapsed line. We inspect the route to separate those faults, because each needs different repair work and can carry a different charge.

Sewer camera inspection

At a sewer camera inspection, we start at an accessible cleanout or remove a suitable drain connection only where needed. We note the pipe material and diameter, then feed a camera through the line to find roots, standing water, cracks, offsets, or a collapse.

Most camera runs take 30 to 60 minutes, depending on line length and access. We use the camera counter to measure the distance to the defect and may mark its surface location before work begins.

We usually handle an accessible cleanout cap or short exposed pipe section during the visit if materials are available. Excavation, lining, work beneath a slab, or repairs needing utility marking or approval are arranged as separate work.

Pipe depth, material, access

The pipe material, its depth, and the route to the break shape the work. PVC at a shallow cleanout has a different repair path from clay or cast iron beneath a driveway, foundation slab, retaining wall, or mature tree.

Access also matters. A clear side yard gives us room for locating and excavation, while fencing, irrigation, landscaping, or a narrow passage adds preparation and restoration.

A camera finding that pinpoints one broken joint is more direct than a long section with root damage or a collapse. Ordered fittings, utility locates, permits, and required inspection sign-off can add steps before the final repair.

Cleanout cap and pipe age

Before calling, locate the cleanout cap if you can and tell us whether it is in the yard, garage, crawlspace, or near the foundation. Tell us the approximate age of the house and whether you know the pipe is PVC, cast iron, or clay.

We also need the rooms and fixtures that back up first, plus whether sewage or water is still moving through any drain. Let us know if the camera or excavation route crosses a locked gate, driveway, deck, landscaped bed, or area with a pet.

Photos of wet ground, a floor drain, or an exposed cleanout can help us plan the inspection. Call Cedar Park Plumbing Company at (512) 986-2512 before a blockage turns into a sewage backup or a break allows more soil to wash away.
